Step 1 · on your phone

Open Smartie Bin on your phone

Scan this with the phone's camera, then log in or register. After that, scan a bin's code below.

The codes point to {{ phone_url }}. Your phone must be on the same Wi-Fi or hotspot as this laptop.

  1. Every smart bin has a QR code sticker. The code holds a link to that bin, for example /scan/bin_001.
  2. The bin's flap stays locked. The consumer scans the code with their phone camera.
  3. The link opens this website. Visitors who aren't signed in log in or register first, then come straight back to the bin.
  4. They tap Open the bin. The bin weighs itself, opens its flap, and its IR sensor watches the textiles go in.
  5. When they're done the flap closes and the bin weighs itself again. If something went in and it weighs at least 100 g, they earn points. Unusually heavy deposits (stones, metal) are flagged instead.
  6. The same person can only deposit at the same bin once every 5 minutes, so repeated scans don't earn extra points.
